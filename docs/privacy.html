Campus Compass Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

Campus Compass is an independent campus navigation guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by California State University or any CSU campus.

Data Collection

Campus Compass does not require an account and does not collect personal information from users through the app.

On-Device Storage

The app may store recently viewed campuses locally on your device to make navigation easier. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Device Permissions

Campus Compass does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Third-Party Services

The app may open Apple Maps or Google Maps links when you choose to get directions. Those services are operated by their respective providers and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Support Contact

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and support request.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit the support page or open a request at github.com/Mazin7d4/Campus-Compass/issues.
